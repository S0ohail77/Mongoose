#Fnd in Mongoose

Model.find() // returns a Query object (thennable)

*Mongoose Queries are not promises. But they have a .then()


 User.find({}).then((res)=>{
    console.log(res);})
    .catch((err)=>{
        console.log(err);
    })

*we can also use conditional statement with Find()


Ex -
 User.find({age : {$gte : 24}}).then((res)=>{
    console.log(res);})
    .catch((err)=>{
        console.log(err);
    })
